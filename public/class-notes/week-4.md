## Week 4: Talking micro:bits & Motion Magic! 📡

### What We Learned This Week

- **Our Mission**: Make micro:bits talk to each other and sense movement!
- How to make micro:bits send messages like walkie-talkies
- Using the built-in motion sensor to detect tilting and shaking
- Saving information like a digital notebook
- Creating cool gadgets that work together
